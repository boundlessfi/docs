# Overview

Boundless Crowdfunding enables **milestone-based funding** with **smart-contract escrow**.

Funds release only after verified delivery.

### What makes it different

Traditional platforms (Kickstarter, Indiegogo, GoFundMe) have weak enforcement.

Boundless enforces delivery through escrowed tranches.

#### The accountability gap

* Creators receive funds upfront.
* Backers have limited recourse.
* Progress is often opaque.
* “Raise-and-run” is possible.

#### The Boundless solution

* **Milestone escrow:** Funds lock in TrustlessWork escrow.
* **Community validation:** Campaigns pass voting before funding opens.
* **Proof of work:** Milestones require verifiable deliverables.
* **Automatic refunds:** If the goal is not met.
* **Dispute resolution:** Neutral arbitration when delivery is contested.
* **Public records:** Pledges, releases, and milestones are on-chain.

### Platform defaults (typical)

* Funding period: **15–60 days**
* Validation period: **7–14 days**
* Funding goals: **5,000–500,000+ XLM**
* Milestones per campaign: **2–10**
* Admin review time: **24–48 hours**
* Assets: **XLM, USDC, EURC**
