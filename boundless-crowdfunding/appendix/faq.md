# FAQ

### What happens if the campaign doesn't reach its goal?

All backers are automatically refunded via escrow.

No fees are charged.

### Can creators change milestones after funding?

Minor adjustments are possible with admin approval.

Major changes require backer consent or re-campaign.

### What if a milestone is rejected?

Creators get detailed feedback.

They can revise and resubmit.

Repeated rejections can trigger dispute.

### How long does milestone review take?

Typically **3–7 business days**.

Urgent reviews can be expedited.

### Can backers get refunds after a campaign is funded?

Only through dispute resolution.

This applies when milestones are not delivered as promised.

### What fees does Boundless charge?

A **5% platform fee** is deducted from creator payments.

Backers pay no fees.
