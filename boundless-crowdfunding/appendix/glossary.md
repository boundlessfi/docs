# Glossary

* **Campaign:** Crowdfunding initiative with defined goals and milestones.
* **Milestone:** Verifiable deliverable tied to a funding tranche.
* **Escrow:** Smart contract holding funds until milestone completion.
* **Tranche:** Portion of total funds released upon milestone approval.
* **Validation:** Community voting process to approve campaigns.
* **Backer:** Individual who pledges funds to a campaign.
* **Pledge:** Commitment of funds to a campaign, locked in escrow.
* **Dispute:** Formal disagreement requiring arbitration.
* **TrustlessWork:** Escrow and arbitration protocol.
