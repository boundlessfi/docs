# Creator journey

###

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/HICX8tcka64" %}

### 1) Initialize

* Open `boundlessfi.xyz`.
* Click **Create Campaign**.
* Fill title, tagline, description, goal, duration.

### 2) Define milestones

For each milestone:

* Deliverables
* % of budget
* Due date
* Success criteria

System enforces:

* Total % = 100%
* Reasonable ordering and timing

### 3) Admin review

* Submit for review.
* If rejected, revise and resubmit.
* If approved, campaign enters validation.

### 4) Community validation

* Promote campaign for votes.
* Monitor vote dashboard.
* Answer questions.
* Avoid major edits that reset votes.

### 5) Launch

* Threshold reached → campaign enters **Campaigning**.
* Funding opens.

### 6) Fundraising

* Monitor pledges and velocity.
* Post updates.
* Engage backers.

### 7) Funding success

* Goal reached → funds lock in escrow.
* Milestone 1 activates.

### 8) Deliver milestones

Per milestone:

* Build.
* Prepare proof.
* Submit for review.

### 9) Respond to review

* Approved → funds release.
* Rejected → revise and resubmit.
* Disputed → provide evidence and enter resolution.

### 10) Complete

* Final milestone approved.
* Campaign state becomes **Completed**.
* Post final update and collect testimonials.
