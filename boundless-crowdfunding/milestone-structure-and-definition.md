# Milestone structure & definition

Milestones define what gets delivered.

They also define when funds release.

### Milestone requirements

Each milestone includes:

* Deliverable description (specific and measurable)
* Funding percentage
* Expected delivery date
* Success criteria
* Proof of work type (code, designs, reports, demos, metrics)

### Configuration rules

* Minimum milestones: **2**
* Maximum milestones: **10**
* Budget allocation must total **100%**
* Milestones must be sequential
* Timeline spacing: at least **2 weeks** between milestones

### Example structures

#### DeFi protocol (goal: 50,000 XLM)

* Milestone 1 (20% — 10,000 XLM): Design & spec
* Milestone 2 (30% — 15,000 XLM): Testnet deployment
* Milestone 3 (30% — 15,000 XLM): Mainnet launch
* Milestone 4 (20% — 10,000 XLM): Community growth

#### Content platform (goal: 15,000 XLM)

* Milestone 1 (35% — 5,250 XLM): First 10 tutorial videos
* Milestone 2 (35% — 5,250 XLM): Next 10 videos
* Milestone 3 (30% — 4,500 XLM): Growth & engagement
