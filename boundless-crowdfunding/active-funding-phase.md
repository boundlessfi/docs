# Active funding phase

Funding opens after validation succeeds.

Pledges deposit directly into escrow.

### Launch sequence

1. Voting threshold reached.
2. Campaign moves to **Campaigning**.
3. Creator receives launch notification.
4. Campaign appears in discovery feeds.
5. Sharing tools activate.
6. Pledge interface goes live.
7. Countdown starts.

### Pledge mechanics

1. Backer opens campaign.
2. Clicks **Back this campaign**.
3. Enters amount (min **10 XLM**).
4. Selects asset (XLM, USDC, EURC).
5. Reviews escrow terms.
6. Confirms with passkey.
7. Funds transfer to escrow.
8. Pledge records on-chain.
9. Backer receives confirmation.

### Progress tracking

* Total raised
* % to goal
* Backer count
* Days remaining
* Funding velocity
* Recent backers feed

### Updates

Creators can post:

* Progress updates
* Announcements
* Milestone previews

Updates support Markdown and media.

Backers get notified.

### Notifications (typical)

To creators:

* First pledge
* 25% / 50% / 75%
* Goal reached
* 24 hours remaining

To backers:

* Pledge confirmed
* Creator updates
* Goal reached
* Campaign ended (success or failure)

### Outcomes

* **Success**: goal reached → funds lock → state becomes **Funded** → milestone 1 activates.
* **Failure**: deadline passes → refunds execute → state becomes **Failed**.
