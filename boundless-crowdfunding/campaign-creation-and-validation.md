# Campaign creation & validation

Creators build a campaign in **Draft**.

Admins gate campaigns into community voting.

### Campaign configuration

#### Basic information

* Title
* One-sentence tagline
* Full description (Markdown, 500–2000 words)
* Category (DeFi, Infrastructure, Gaming, etc.)
* Funding goal (XLM/USDC/EURC)
* Campaign duration (15–60 days)
* Banner image and media gallery

#### Team information

* Creator profile (auto-populated)
* Team members (names, roles, links)
* Past projects and credentials
* Social links (X, Discord, website)

#### Milestones (2–10)

Each milestone includes:

* Title and description
* Deliverables
* % of budget
* Timeline
* Acceptance criteria

Rules:

* Total milestone percentages must equal **100%**.

#### Budget breakdown

* Total requested amount
* Allocation per milestone
* Budget justification
* Expected expenses

#### Rewards (optional)

* Backer tiers
* NFT badges
* Exclusive access

### Admin review (before voting)

Admins check:

* Completeness
* Realistic milestones
* Clear deliverables and criteria
* Appropriate goal for scope
* Policy compliance
* Team credibility

Outcomes:

* **Approve** → state becomes **Validated**.
* **Reject** → creator receives feedback and can resubmit.

Target turnaround: **24–48 hours**.

### Common rejection reasons

* Unclear milestones
* Unrealistic goals
* Missing critical details
* Policy violations
* Team credibility concerns
* Duplicate campaigns
