# Grant types

Boundless supports four grant types.

Each type has different deliverable expectations.

### Research grants

Fund research that advances blockchain and payments knowledge.

Research prioritizes open publication.

**Typical budget:** 5,000–20,000 XLM.

**Duration:** 2–6 months.

#### Common deliverables

* Research papers or whitepapers.
* Technical reports with recommendations.
* Open datasets.
* Survey results and analysis.
* Feasibility studies.
* Literature reviews.

#### Example topics

* Payment channels.
* Scalability limits and mitigations.
* Privacy and ZK integration.
* Bridge security analysis.
* Tokenomics modeling.
* UX adoption barriers.

#### Evaluation criteria

* **Academic rigor (30%).** Methodology, grounding, validity.
* **Ecosystem relevance (25%).** Applicability to Stellar/Web3.
* **Team expertise (20%).** Publications and credentials.
* **Feasibility (15%).** Scope, timeline, resourcing.
* **Open access (10%).** Commitment to public release.

#### Milestone example

Grant: 15,000 XLM for payment channel research.

* Milestone 1 (25%, 3,750 XLM).
  * 4 weeks.
  * Literature review and methodology.
* Milestone 2 (40%, 6,000 XLM).
  * 8 weeks.
  * Data collection and analysis.
* Milestone 3 (35%, 5,250 XLM).
  * 4 weeks.
  * Final paper, presentation, open dataset.

### Development grants

Fund tools, protocols, apps, and infrastructure.

Most outputs should be open-source.

**Typical budget:** 10,000–50,000 XLM.

**Duration:** 3–12 months.

#### Common deliverables

* Public code repositories.
* Technical docs and API references.
* Testnet and mainnet deployments.
* SDKs, libraries, CLIs.
* Audited Soroban contracts.
* CI/CD and test suites.

#### Example projects

* SDKs for Soroban interaction.
* Smart contract debugging tools.
* DEX and DeFi protocols.
* Mobile wallets.
* Explorers and analytics.
* Cross-chain bridges.

#### Evaluation criteria

* **Technical merit (30%).** Architecture, innovation, code standards.
* **Team capability (25%).** Track record and execution.
* **Ecosystem impact (20%).** Gaps filled and user potential.
* **Open-source commitment (15%).** License, docs, engagement.
* **Budget justification (10%).** Costs and milestone breakdown.

#### Milestone example

Grant: 30,000 XLM for a Stellar mobile wallet.

* Milestone 1 (20%, 6,000 XLM).
  * 1 month.
  * Spec, UI/UX, architecture.
* Milestone 2 (35%, 10,500 XLM).
  * 2.5 months.
  * Testnet MVP, repo, 70% test coverage.
* Milestone 3 (30%, 9,000 XLM).
  * 2 months.
  * Mainnet launch, audit, app stores.
* Milestone 4 (15%, 4,500 XLM).
  * 1.5 months.
  * Docs and adoption targets.

### Community grants

Support education, events, content, and community building.

These grants drive awareness and adoption.

**Typical budget:** 2,000–15,000 XLM.

**Duration:** 1–6 months.

#### Common deliverables

* Tutorials, articles, videos.
* Event reports.
* Translations.
* Moderation and ambassador work.
* Social campaigns.
* Podcasts.

#### Example projects

* Stellar tutorial video series.
* Local meetup series.
* Documentation translation.
* Newsletter.

#### Evaluation criteria

* **Impact potential (30%).** Reach and growth metrics.
* **Track record (25%).** Portfolio and past work.
* **Audience fit (20%).** Underserved regions and niches.
* **Quality standards (15%).** Accuracy and professionalism.
* **Sustainability (10%).** Post-grant plan.

#### Milestone example

Grant: 8,000 XLM for a tutorial video series.

* Milestone 1 (30%, 2,400 XLM).
  * 6 weeks.
  * First 5 videos + scripts.
* Milestone 2 (40%, 3,200 XLM).
  * 10 weeks.
  * Next 10 videos.
* Milestone 3 (30%, 2,400 XLM).
  * 6 weeks.
  * Final 5 videos + promotion.

### Ecosystem grants

Fund high-impact, larger-scope initiatives.

Timelines are longer.

**Typical budget:** 20,000–100,000+ XLM.

**Duration:** 6–18 months.

#### Common deliverables

* Major protocols.
* Core infrastructure.
* Cross-chain solutions.
* Developer platforms.
* Enterprise integrations.
* Governance systems.

#### Example projects

* AMM and DeFi primitives.
* Indexers and query services.
* Remittance rails.
* Bridges.
* IDEs and training platforms.

#### Evaluation criteria

* **Strategic value (35%).** Critical gap and long-term value.

#### Milestone example

Grant: 75,000 XLM for a DEX protocol.

* Milestone 1 (15%, 11,250 XLM).
  * 2 months.
  * Protocol spec and model.
* Milestone 2 (25%, 18,750 XLM).
  * 4 months.
  * Testnet contracts and test suite.
* Milestone 3 (20%, 15,000 XLM).
  * 3 months.
  * Frontend, API, testnet demo.
* Milestone 4 (25%, 18,750 XLM).
  * 3 months.
  * Audit and mainnet launch.
* Milestone 5 (15%, 11,250 XLM).
  * 3 months.
  * Adoption and growth targets.
