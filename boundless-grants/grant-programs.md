# Grant programs

Programs bundle grants around a theme.

They also define budgets and timelines.

### Program structure

A program usually defines:

* Program name.
* Total budget.
* Grant size range.
* Focus areas.
* Application open and close dates.
* Review timeline.
* Target number of awards.
* Program-level success metrics.

### Recurring programs

Recurring programs make funding predictable.

#### Quarterly developer grants

* **Budget:** 300,000 XLM per quarter.
* **Focus:** tools, protocols, applications.
* **Applications:** month 1.
* **Decisions:** month 2.

#### Bi-annual research grants

* **Budget:** 150,000 XLM per half.
* **Focus:** academic and industry research.
* **Applications:** January and July.
* **Decisions:** February and August.

#### Monthly community grants

* **Budget:** 50,000 XLM per month.
* **Focus:** education, events, content.
* **Applications:** rolling.
* **Decisions:** within 2 weeks.

### Special programs

* Themed initiatives.
  * Example: “Privacy Tech Grant”.
* Partnership programs.
  * Co-funded with ecosystem partners.
* Emergency grants.
  * Fast-track for critical infra and security.
* Matching grants.
  * Amplify community crowdfunding.
* Seasonal campaigns.
  * Aligned with ecosystem events.
