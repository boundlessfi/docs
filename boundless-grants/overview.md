# Overview

Boundless Grants enables direct ecosystem funding.

Payments are tied to verified milestones.

Milestones are enforced by escrow smart contracts.

### The grant problem

Traditional grant programs break in predictable ways:

* **Upfront distribution.** Funds go out before delivery.
* **Accountability gaps.** No enforcement when milestones slip.
* **Manual tracking.** Managers chase updates.
* **Opaque selection.** Favoritism risk.
* **Wasted budgets.** Partial delivery with no recourse.
* **No verifiable history.** Past success is hard to prove.

### The Boundless approach

* **Milestone escrow.** Grant funds are locked in TrustlessWork.
* **Automatic enforcement.** Contracts release funds only on approval.
* **Transparent evaluation.** Public criteria and weighted scoring.
* **On-chain reputation.** Delivery builds credibility.
* **Dispute resolution.** Neutral arbitration when needed.
* **Public tracking.** Progress stays visible to the community.

### Key features

* Four grant types.
  * Research.
  * Development.
  * Community.
  * Ecosystem.
* Recurring programs.
  * Quarterly and seasonal tracks.
* Expert review.
  * 3–5 domain reviewers per application.
* Milestone structure.
  * Usually 2–6 milestones.
* Cross-module reputation.
  * Delivery improves outcomes across Boundless.
  * See [Reputation system](../boundless-bounty/core-concepts/reputation-system.md).
* Impact tracking.
  * On-chain and off-chain metrics.
* Program templates.
  * Reuse structures for recurring initiatives.

### Target users

#### Grant applicants

* Researchers.
* Developers.
* Community builders.
* Projects seeking ecosystem support.
* Teams working on larger initiatives.

#### Grant committees

* Ecosystem partners.
* Technical experts.
* Community representatives.
* Grant managers.

### Typical parameters

* Grant types: 4.
* Typical size: **2,000–100,000 XLM**.
* Milestones: **2–6**.
* Review time: **2–4 weeks**.
* Program cadence: quarterly, seasonal, and special.
* Escrow: TrustlessWork smart contracts.
