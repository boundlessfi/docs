# Glossary

* **Grant program:** Structured funding initiative with a defined budget, timeline, and focus.
* **Milestone:** Verifiable deliverable tied to a portion of grant funding.
* **Escrow:** TrustlessWork smart contract holding grant funds until delivery.
* **Grantee:** Individual or team receiving grant funding.
* **Grant committee:** Group responsible for evaluating and approving grants.
* **Proof of work:** Evidence submitted to demonstrate milestone completion.
