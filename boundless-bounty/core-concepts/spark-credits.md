# Spark Credits

Spark Credits prevent spam.

They also reward high-quality participation.

### Defaults

* Cost: **1 Spark Credit** per application.
* Starting balance: **3**.
* Max capacity: **10**.
* Non-transferable and tied to your account.

### How you earn credits

* **Bi-weekly recharge:** +3 credits on the **1st** and **15th**.
* **Bounty completion:** +2 credits.
* **High-quality work (5/5):** +3 credits.
* **Fast completion:** +1 bonus when done within 50% of deadline.
* **Referrals:** +1 when a referred user completes 1 bounty.
* **Valid bug report:** +2 credits.

### When credits are restored

* Applied but not selected (Application model): +1.
* Competition participant who didn’t win: +1.
* Bounty canceled after you apply: full refund.
* Unfair rejection after appeal: +1.

### How it shows in the UI

* Battery-style charge icon.
* Numeric label like `7/10`.
* Next recharge date.
* Low-balance warning under 3.
