# Surge events (Lightning Rounds)

Surge events are periodic bounty drops.

The current working name is **Lightning Rounds**.

They match the Spark Credits theme.

### Format

* Frequency: every **10 days**.
* Duration: **48–72 hours** for new claims and applications.
* Volume: **20–50 bounties** released at once.
* Mix: all claiming models and skill categories.
* Bonus: **+1 Spark Credit** for participating.

### Timeline

* Day -3: teaser.
* Day -1: preview list.
* Day 0: round starts.
* Day 0–2: claiming and applying.
* Day 3: round closes. Winners announced.
* Day 3–13: work completion window.

### Curation guidelines

* Wide skill range.
* Multiple budget tiers.
* At least 5 bounties per claiming model.
* 30% newcomer-friendly (reputation level 1).
