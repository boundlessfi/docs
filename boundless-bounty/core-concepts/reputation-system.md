# Reputation system

Reputation helps projects pick reliable contributors.

It’s designed to be on-chain and verifiable.

### Score factors (weights)

* **Bounties completed:** 40%
* **Quality rating:** 30%
* **Timeliness:** 15%
* **Collaboration:** 10%
* **Community contribution:** 5%

### Levels

* Level 1: Rookie (0–100)
* Level 2: Contributor (101–300)
* Level 3: Specialist (301–600)
* Level 4: Expert (601–1000)
* Level 5: Master (1001+)

### Benefits (examples)

* Higher levels unlock higher-value bounties.
* High levels get direct invitations and priority support.
* Capacity can increase over time (example: Level 3 adds +1 max Spark Credit).

### What hurts reputation

* Abandoning claimed bounties.
* Late delivery.
* Poor ratings.
* Fraud or plagiarism.

### Skill-specific reputation

You can have different levels per skill.

Example: Level 4 in Design and Level 2 in Writing.
