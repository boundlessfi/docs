# Leaderboards

Leaderboards create healthy competition.

They also surface trustworthy projects and top talent.

### 1) Contributor leaderboard

Scoring inputs:

* Total earnings.
* Bounties completed.
* Average rating.
* Completion speed.
* Diversity across models and skills.

Views:

* All-time.
* Yearly.
* Monthly.
* Event-specific (Lightning Round).
* Skill-specific.

### 2) Sponsor / maintainer leaderboard

Scoring inputs:

* Bounties posted.
* Total value distributed.
* Contributor satisfaction.
* Review response time.
* Completion rate.
* Fair practice signals (disputes, clarity).

### 3) Project leaderboard

Scoring inputs:

* Bounty activity and consistency.
* Community engagement.
* Budget commitment.
* Innovation and variety.
* Contributor growth.
* Transparency and verification.

### Anti-gaming rules (examples)

* Minimum thresholds to appear.
* Quality weighting.
* Sybil detection.
* Dispute penalties.
* Self-dealing flags.

### Privacy

* Default: appear if you rank high enough.
* Opt-out hides you from public boards.
* Anonymous mode can show rank without identity.
