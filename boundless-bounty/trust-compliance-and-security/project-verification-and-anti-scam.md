# Project verification and anti-scam

Projects need to be accountable.

Contributors need to feel safe applying.

### Project verification levels

#### Level 0 — Unverified

* Email verification only.
* Lower budget limits.
* No verification badge.

#### Level 1 — Verified project

* Org details and social proof.
* Team info (names and roles).
* Basic project docs.
* “Verified” badge.

#### Level 2 — Trusted project

* Legal entity verification.
* Authorized representative KYC.
* Detailed milestones and timelines.
* Higher limits and premium visibility.

### Milestone accountability

* Public roadmap.
* Quarterly progress updates.
* Milestones marked as not started, in progress, completed, delayed.
* Auto-flag after 30 days of delay.

### Escrow protection

* Bounty budgets are locked in escrow.
* Funds release only on approval.
* Funds return to projects only when rules allow.

### Deposits and insurance (concept)

* Unverified projects can post with a deposit held back.
* Deposits can be forfeited on fraud or abandonment.
* An insurance fund can cover verified contributor losses.

### Red flags

* High rejection rates.
* Slow reviews.
* Missed milestones.
* Multiple disputes.
* Low contributor ratings.

### Community reporting

* Report button on projects and bounties.
* Evidence-based review by the Boundless team.
