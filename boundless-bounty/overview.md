# Overview

Boundless Bounty is a Stellar-native bounty platform.

It pays contributors in XLM.

It supports technical and non-technical work.

### Key differentiators

* **Inclusive by design.** Design, writing, marketing, research, community, and more.
* **Four claiming models.** Match the work type and budget.
* **Spark Credits.** Block spam. Reward quality participation.
* **Passkey wallets.** No seed phrases. No wallet setup friction.
* **Surge events.** Periodic high-volume bounty drops.
* **Unified ecosystem.** Works alongside Boundless hackathons, grants, and crowdfunding.

### What you can do

#### Participants (bounty hunters)

* Find work across 15+ skill categories.
* Apply using Spark Credits.
* Get paid directly to your Stellar wallet.
* Build a verifiable reputation.

#### Projects (sponsors and ecosystems)

* Post bounties with escrowed budgets.
* Choose a model that fits the task.
* Review applicants and submissions.
* Track performance and spend.
