# FAQ

### How do I get started?

Sign up at https://boundlessfi.xyz/auth.

Then complete your profile and browse bounties.

### What skills can earn bounties?

Any Web3-adjacent skill.

Design, writing, coding, research, marketing, translation, video, community.

### How do Spark Credits work?

Most applications cost 1 credit.

You earn credits via recharges and completion bonuses.

### What if I run out of Spark Credits?

Wait for the next recharge.

Or complete bounties and community actions to earn more.

### Which bounty model should I choose?

* Single Claim: urgent and well-defined.
* Application: expertise-heavy and high stakes.
* Competition: creative and subjective.
* Multi-winner: large and divisible.

### How long does payment take?

On approval, escrow releases immediately.

Stellar transfers are near-instant.

### Is KYC required?

Not for small bounties.

Higher tiers and off-ramping require verification.

### Can I work on multiple bounties?

Yes.

You still need credits and must meet each bounty’s rules.
