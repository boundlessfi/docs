# Glossary

* **Bounty:** A paid task posted by a project.
* **Claiming model:** The rules that decide how work is awarded.
* **Spark Credits:** Credits required to apply for certain bounties.
* **Lightning Round:** A high-volume surge event.
* **Reputation:** A score that reflects reliability and quality.
* **Passkey:** Biometric login via WebAuthn.
* **Smart account:** Wallet abstraction that avoids seed phrases.
* **Escrow:** Contract-controlled funds, released on approval.
* **XLM:** Stellar Lumens. The currency used for payouts.
* **Milestone:** A stage in a multi-winner bounty.
* **Off-ramp:** Convert XLM to fiat and withdraw to a bank.
* **KYC:** Identity verification.
* **Sybil attack:** Many fake accounts used to game incentives.
